Project O-Seal

Lead Engineer Oct 2025 – Present Provisional patent

A device that seals pipe leaks from the inside — repairing aging infrastructure without digging it up.

What it is

O-Seal is a model I designed and prototyped to stop pipe leakages from inside the pipe. Instead of excavating to reach a failure point, the device travels to the leak and seals it in place — cheaper, faster, and far less disruptive than conventional repair. You can see the project at oseal.co.

Where it's gone

  • Featured in the Right Here, Right Now Global Youth Climate Summit, sponsored by the University of Oxford.
  • Top 2% of teams worldwide in the Blue Ocean competition.
  • Alternate Finalist in the CONRAD Challenge.
  • Provisional patent acquired — a formal patent is pending.

What I'm doing now

I'm collecting performance data on the model and writing a paper to present the broader idea of in-pipe repair — where it works, how it scales, and what it could mean for water systems that lose enormous volumes to undetected leaks.

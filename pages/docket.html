On the docket

Things I want to start

A running list of ideas I've queued up. Some of these will move to "in progress"; some won't. We'll see.

October 2025

A drone-based flight system to magnetically support items that don't need to be lifted.

Using drones and batteries to create an electromagnetic system that can lift backpacks, laptops, and more to make it easier for people to transport items. It also would look SO COOL (it'd have CV to track people and have their items follow them, and could also act as a mobile laptop stand?)

June 2025

A software to automatically configure CNC files.

Figure out an algorithm and/or formula to take any file, and, given several parameters (i.e. drill bits, toolpath possibility space, etc) generate a GCODE file without forcing people to manually parameterize and generate them.

May 2025

Music reading app

An app for musicians to use to scan music and have it automatically scroll when playing, host built in tuners and metronomes, store margin notes, and (tentative) have the option to play certain sections, as well as listen to a musician's rendition and critique tempo, intonation, dynamic, etc.

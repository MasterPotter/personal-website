How the work happens

I worked closely with Professor Jiasun Li and a small research team out of Fairfax, Virginia, learning to take a question from raw data all the way to a written, defensible result. The finance topics were new territory for me — which was the point. The transferable skill is the research process itself: framing a question, gathering and cleaning data, and communicating findings clearly.

That same research instinct shows up across my hardware work, too — from characterizing optical tweezers to collecting performance data on Project O-Seal.
